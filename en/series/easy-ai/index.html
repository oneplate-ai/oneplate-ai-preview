Digestible AI · Plate Six

How to Ask AI for
the Answer You Need

When you are not sure what to ask an AI, do not look for a magic sentence.
Describe your goal, situation, format, and limits, then review the answer step by step.

A prompt is a request you send to AI.

A prompt is a question or instruction you send to an AI service.
A one-line question is a fine place to start, but the AI does not automatically know the goal or conditions that you have only kept in your head.
Giving it a little more information can make the answer fit your situation better.

A good prompt is not a magic sentence that guarantees a correct answer.
It gives the AI more clues about what to do and makes the answer easier for you to review.

Start by stating your goal. OpenAI guidance

You might begin with a short request like this.

Tell me what to pack for camping.

This can produce an answer, but the AI does not know whether you are taking a day trip or staying overnight, or who the trip is for.
Start by saying what you are trying to do.

I am preparing for my first overnight camping trip. Tell me what I need to pack.

When you state the goal, the AI has a clearer direction for its answer.
The same approach works for a summary, a travel plan, or a first draft of an email: say what you are trying to accomplish first.

Add the situation and conditions. Google guidance

Different seasons, group sizes, and ways of traveling call for different camping preparations.
Briefly add the information that should shape the answer.

Two adults are taking their first overnight camping trip by car on an autumn weekend. Tell me what we need to pack.

You do not need to provide a lot of personal information.
It is safer not to paste sensitive details such as your home address, phone number, government ID number, or private company material into a prompt.
Choose only the conditions the AI needs to answer your question.

Choose the shape of the answer you want.

AI can answer with a table, a list, a short summary, or step-by-step instructions.
Telling it the format you want can reduce the work of reorganizing the answer later.

List seven essential items in a numbered list. Give one sentence explaining why each item is needed.

For easier writing, you could ask, “Explain this in three short paragraphs for a child.”
For meeting notes, you could ask, “Make a table with decisions, owners, and due dates.”

State what to leave out and how to check the answer.

If an answer is too long or includes things you do not need, add what it should not do and the standard it should meet.

Leave out expensive equipment recommendations, and mark items a first-time camper might easily forget as “check.”

You do not need to write a perfect request on the first try.
It is normal to read the result and improve one part at a time, such as “shorten the list to five items” or “adjust this for rainy weather.”

Do not rely on an AI answer alone for important information. Anthropic guidance

Even a detailed prompt cannot guarantee that every AI answer is correct.
Check important information such as prices, stock, reservations, safety instructions, medical guidance, legal matters, or financial matters against official information or a reliable original source.

Asking AI to include sources can help.
But checking that a link exists and applies to your situation is still a person's job.

One-line takeaway.

For a more useful AI answer, state your goal, situation, format, and limits, then verify important information yourself.

This draft was prepared on October 8, 2026 after checking official prompting guidance from OpenAI, Google, and Anthropic.
Features and responses can vary by model, settings, and time.
